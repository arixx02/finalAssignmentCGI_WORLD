   GAIN split into intervenable levers vs fixed attributes.

5. COMBINE -> REVENUE-AT-RISK & VALIDATION - risk_i = p(churn_i) x rev_i.

--- C.6 Problems found and fixed during dry-runs ---

 1. KeyError: 'feature' in figure 05 (melt without var_name)
    -> melt(..., var_name="feature", value_name="value")
 2. Notebook cell timed out (>300 s) training the revenue model
    -> set tree_method="hist" explicitly (43 s instead of >300 s)
 3. Threshold chosen by max-F1 gave a misleading 0.33 cut-off (accuracy 0.595)
    -> report metrics at 0.50 + add the campaign operating point (top 10% of
       scores) with recall/precision, marked on the ROC curve
 4. mean_squared_error(squared=False) removed in scikit-learn >=1.6 (Colab)
    -> np.sqrt(mean_squared_error(...))
 5. seaborn palette without hue deprecated/removed in 0.14 (Colab)
    -> pass hue=..., then remove the legend; set_xticks + set_xticklabels
 6. "$58.21" etc. in markdown would be parsed by MathJax as inline math
    -> auto-escape $ -> \$ in markdown cells that contain no $$ block; split the
       $$...$$ formula into its own cell
 7. A % comment inside $$...$$ silently hid the formula legend
    -> rewrote the equation cell with a proper legend line
 8. Cells missing nbformat id fields -> generator now assigns unique ids
 9. Lift table printed ROI rounded to whole numbers (0.0 / 1.0)
    -> round money and ROI separately
10. Lever text originally claimed "falling usage" as an offer driver - data did
    not support it -> rewrote the recommendation around overage, equipment age
    and the 1-2-year renewal window; documented the rejected hypothesis
11. Base-rate table dropped the 1,972 customers with zero minutes used
    (mou_Mean = 0), so its percentages disagreed with figure 13 (14.98% vs
    14.7% high-overage) -> treat zero-minute customers as "no overage"
    (fillna) so both tables use the same definition
12. Figure 12 was unreadable on a slide: 144 overlapping value annotations and
    a staircase of black boxes -> redrew with pcolormesh, values in $ thousands,
    a single black BREAK-EVEN CONTOUR LINE, and a gold star marking the base case
13. Figure 12 axes printed raw decimals (0.14, 0.3) instead of percentages
    -> PercentFormatter(1.0) on both axes
14. ROC legend said "recall 16% at 4% contacted" - the 4% is the FALSE-POSITIVE
    rate, not the share contacted -> relabelled "catches 16% of churners,
    4% false-alarm rate"
